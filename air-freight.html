What it is

Air Freight Consolidation groups shipments onto the fastest available routing out of Lagos, so time-sensitive cargo moves quickly without you having to book and manage an entire aircraft's worth of space yourself. It's the service to reach for when a delay is more expensive than the freight bill.

Who it's for

Built for cargo that can't wait.

Time-sensitive shipments

Orders, parts or stock that need to land on a fixed date.

High-value or urgent cargo

Goods where careful, priority handling matters.

Documents & samples

Smaller, urgent consignments that sea freight can't serve.

How it works

From booking to arrival.

01Booking & consolidation. Your cargo is grouped onto the fastest suitable routing.
02Airport handling. Cargo is staged, documented and dispatched.
03In-transit status updates. You know where things stand, booking to touchdown.
04Arrival & handover: paired with our Customs Clearance and Door to Door services if you need it collected and delivered too.
