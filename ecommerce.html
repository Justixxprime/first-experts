What it is

E-commerce Logistics packages our core freight, customs and delivery services around what online sellers actually need: getting inventory in from your supplier, through Nigerian customs, and either to your own storage or straight out to customers, on a schedule that keeps up with orders coming in.

How it works

From supplier to customer, one coordinated route.

01Inventory booked and consolidated at origin, via Air Freight or Sea Freight depending on volume and timing.
02Customs clearance handled on arrival through our Customs Clearance service.
03Road transport to your warehouse, fulfillment partner, or directly to customers, via Haulage or Door to Door.
04Status shared with you as each shipment moves, so restocking isn't a guessing game.

E-commerce Logistics: FAQ

Do you deliver directly to my customers, or just to my warehouse?

Either. Tell us your preferred setup when you request a quote and we'll route accordingly.

Can I set up a recurring shipment schedule?

Yes. Once your route and paperwork are set up, reordering freight on the same lane is straightforward. Ask us about it directly.

Is this only for large sellers?

No. Both FCL/LCL sea freight and air freight are available, so shipment size scales with what you're actually ordering.
