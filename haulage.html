What it is

Haulage is road transport booked as its own service, not just the last leg of a Door to Door shipment. If your cargo is moving between cities in Nigeria, or overland by road to a neighbouring country, we handle the trucking, scheduling and paperwork directly, on its own timeline.

Where it fits

On its own, or alongside air and sea.

Standalone road freight

Domestic haulage between Nigerian cities, or cross-border by road, booked and billed as its own shipment.

Paired with air or sea

The same road network also carries cargo that's just landed by Air Freight or Sea Freight onward to its final stop, coordinated through Door to Door.

Scheduling & documentation

Route, transit time and paperwork confirmed up front, so there's nothing to chase once the truck is loaded.

Haulage: FAQ

Is Haulage only for cargo already on an international shipment?

No. You can book Haulage as a standalone road shipment, entirely separate from any air or sea freight.

Which routes do you cover by road?

See the Routes page for lanes currently on our schedule, or ask us about a route that isn't listed.

How is Haulage different from Door to Door?

Door to Door bundles pickup, international transit, customs and final delivery under one booking. Haulage is the road transport on its own, for shipments that don't need the full international journey.
