What it is

Door to Door is the road transport and coordination layer that sits around your air or sea shipment. Pickup at origin, handling through transit, clearance, and final delivery to your receiving dock, so you're dealing with one team instead of a different contact at every stage.

The journey

From pickup to your dock.

01Pickup coordinated at origin.
02Transit. Air, sea or road, as booked.
03Customs clearance handled on your behalf, via our Customs Clearance service.
04Road transport to your receiving dock in Lagos or beyond.
05Delivery confirmation sent back to you.

Why Door to Door with us

One point of contact

A single team coordinating your shipment end to end. Not a different handler at each stage.

Road transport included

Pickup and last-mile delivery are part of the service, not a separate booking.

Works with air or sea

Pairs with either Air Freight or Sea Freight for the international leg.

Door to Door: FAQ

Can you deliver directly to my address?

Yes. That's the core of this service. We coordinate everything from port or airport of origin straight to your receiving dock.

Do you handle deliveries outside Lagos?

Tell us your delivery location and we'll confirm coverage and timing when you request a quote.

Does this include customs clearance?

Yes, clearance is coordinated as part of the journey through our Customs Clearance service.
