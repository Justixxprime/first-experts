Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

How do I get a shipping quote?

Reach out through our Contact page with details of what you're shipping, where it's going, and roughly when. We'll confirm rate and schedule within hours.

Yes. Our licensed clearance team handles it directly at Nigerian ports and airports, so there's no third-party delay added to your shipment.
