Privacy Policy.

Effective August 2026. First Experts Logistics Plc ("First Experts," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have over it.

1. Who we are

First Experts Logistics Plc is a freight forwarding company based in Lagos, Nigeria, at 1 Murtala Muhammed International Airport Road, Opposite Hajji Camp. We are the "data controller" for the information described in this policy: the organisation responsible for deciding how and why it is used.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal information you choose to give us directly, through the Quote and Contact forms on this site. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, and details about a shipment you want moved: origin, destination, cargo description, weight, and preferred timing. We do not collect personal information about you from any other source, and we do not use tracking technology to build a profile of your activity across other websites.

3. How we use your information

We use what you submit for one purpose: to respond to you, prepare a quote, and arrange and manage your shipment if you choose to book with us. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without your separate consent, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone, under any circumstances.

4. Who we share it with

Your enquiry is delivered to our team by a form processing service acting on our instructions, solely to route your message to us by email. It is not shared with any other party. We may disclose information where required by law, such as in response to a valid request from a Nigerian regulatory or law enforcement authority.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. It does not currently run visitor analytics. If that changes in the future, this policy and the site will be updated to disclose it and, where required, ask for your consent first.

6. How long we keep your information

We retain enquiry and shipment information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet our legal and accounting obligations, and resolve any dispute, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information we hold against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information to a high standard.

8. Your rights

You may ask us, at any time: what personal information we hold about you, to correct information that is inaccurate, or to delete your information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law.

9. International shipments

Because we arrange international freight, shipment-related information you provide may be shared with carriers, customs authorities, and agents in the countries your cargo moves through, strictly as needed to complete that shipment.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The effective date at the top of this page will always show when it was last revised.

11. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your personal information, contact us at info4u@firstexpertslogistics.com, by phone at 0703 134 1072, or by post at 1 Murtala Muhammed International Airport Road, Opposite Hajji Camp, Lagos, Nigeria.

This policy was prepared for First Experts Logistics and reviewed for plain-language clarity. As with any company's legal documents, we recommend periodic review by qualified counsel as the business and applicable law evolve.
